## Known Issues / Flags

**DECIDED 2026-11-16: Q19 field duplicate RPD (`functions/assess_rpd.R`, `tests/test_assess_rpd.R`).** The project operates under QAPP v3 (2023, Addendum April 2024); the QAPP is updated about every 5 years and each update incorporates the most recent standards. Q19 reports QAPP v3 as the primary result and ADEC 2026 guidance beside it, and its text must say so. QAPP v3 rule: RPD is used only when one or both results are above the LOQ and one or both are at least 2x LOQ (else Not evaluable). ADEC 2026: if either result is below LOQ, skip when the other is under 2x LOQ, fail when it is at least 2x LOQ; both at or above LOQ are evaluated; both below LOQ are Not evaluable (not addressed by ADEC; our reading). Thresholds (QAPP Table 5, confirmed by the user): nitrate/nitrite 25, total phosphorus 25, TSS 5, otherwise 20. Fecal coliform has no QAPP RPD limit (`**` in Table 5); the 20 is a project default, and counts per 100 mL are compared directly (user confirmed). Duplicate pairs are matched from the data (site, date, characteristic, fraction, method, unit), not from a per-year `field_dup_sites` sheet. Non-detects arrive as Result Value 0 and count as below LOQ. Test-fixture result (2025, 80 pairs): the rules differ only on pairs with both results above LOQ but under 2x LOQ (QAPP skips, ADEC evaluates).

**DESIGN 2026-11-16: `assign_qc_status()` (not yet written).** Result Status ID is only Accepted/Rejected (`topics/adec_and_docs.md`). Lab QC fails reject same-analyte, same-method sample results in the same batch. **DECIDED 2026-11-16 (blank scope) and FOUND (detection limits).** A failing blank rejects same-batch, same-analyte, same-method sample results that are detected and below 5x the blank result (project convention; proposed multiple 5, user may override; Q20 text must say the QAPP is silent and the 5x convention is the project's own). Blank criterion: QAPP rule primary with the ADEC rule shown beside it (as Q19). SGS terminology note (`DL, LOD, LOQ Interpretation.pdf`, SGS, Nov 2013): DL < LOD < LOQ; results between DL and LOQ are J; results below DL are reported as the LOD value with U. So the QAPP blank rule (at or below DL) equals "any non-U result in a blank fails". The raw SGS EDD has explicit `DL`, `LOD`, `LOQ` and `REPDL` columns; `REPDL` equals `LOD`, and in the 2025 EDD LOD is 0.75 x LOQ (not 1/2 x LOQ as the SGS note and QAPP Table 7 say; ask SGS). Problems found: (a) `lab_qc` lacks DL and LOQ; (b) `format_wqx.R` (about line 207) writes `Result Detection Limit Type 2 = "Method Detection Level"` using the LOD value, but the true method detection level is the EDD `DL` (about 0.3 x LOQ); (c) `assess_lab_qc()` blank rule uses `repdl`, which is the LOD, whereas ADEC's "reporting limit" is the LOQ. The 2025 report's `U` definition ("not detected at or above the LOD") is not SGS's definition.

DECIDED 2026-11-16: blank multiple is 5x, stored in `config.yml` as `qc: blank_multiple: 5` (read by `assign_qc_status()`, never hardcoded). **Q20 text to include in `qaqc.qmd` beside the blank results (draft):** "The QAPP (v3) requires laboratory blanks to be at or below the detection limit but does not say how a failed blank affects associated sample results. This project therefore applies its own convention: when a blank fails, sample results in the same laboratory batch, for the same analyte and method, that were detected and are less than 5 times the blank result are designated Rejected. Results at or above 5 times the blank are retained because the blank could contribute at most about 20% of the reported value. The multiple (`qc: blank_multiple` in `config.yml`) is a KWF project convention, not a QAPP, ADEC or EPA requirement. The ADEC 2026 rule (a blank fails at or above the LOQ) is shown beside the QAPP rule for comparison; it does not affect status. Rejected results remain in the public record." Basis for choosing 5x over 10x: fewer rejected results (2025 fixture, batch proxy: about 91 vs 124 sample results), at the cost of a weaker contamination guard; no published source was found for either multiple.
